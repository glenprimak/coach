# Prompt: Market Update

**Purpose:** Turn logged market data into content that positions Glen as the agent who
actually tracks the numbers closely — never generic "market update" filler.

## Required Reading Before Writing

1. `inputs/market-data.md` — the only allowed source for any figure used in this content
2. `knowledge/markets.md` — neighborhood framing and context
3. `knowledge/voice.md`
4. `knowledge/audience.md` — market updates serve sellers, investors, and relocation
   buyers differently; pick the angle deliberately

## Steps

1. Confirm the data being used exists in `inputs/market-data.md` with a source and
   date. If the needed figures aren't logged, stop and request them — do not estimate
   or recall general market trends from memory.
2. Identify the single most interesting, specific finding in the data — not "the market
   is strong," but the actual specific movement (e.g., a real inventory shift, a real
   days-on-market change, in a named neighborhood). Genuinely flat or unremarkable data
   is still worth reporting honestly — don't manufacture drama that isn't there.
3. Write the update leading with that finding, not with a throat-clearing "here's your
   monthly market update."
4. Translate the number into what it actually means for the reader (a seller deciding
   whether to list now, a buyer wondering if they have negotiating room, an investor
   evaluating yield) — data without interpretation is just a spreadsheet.
5. Choose the angle by persona:
   - **For sellers:** what this means for pricing and timing decisions
   - **For relocation/waterfront buyers:** what this means for competition and
     negotiating leverage
   - **For investors:** what this means for entry pricing and hold strategy
6. Produce format-specific versions as needed: LinkedIn post (data-forward), Instagram
   carousel (one stat per slide), email (fuller narrative), blog post (deepest version
   with full context).

## Output Format

```
**Headline finding:** [the one sentence someone would repeat to a friend]
**Source & date:** [from inputs/market-data.md]
**Persona/angle:** [from knowledge/audience.md]

[Content, in requested format(s)]

**CTA:** [e.g., "want the full breakdown for your neighborhood? reply and I'll send it
over"]
```

## Output Location

Save to `outputs/market-updates/YYYY-MM-DD-[area-slug].md`.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Every number sourced directly from `inputs/market-data.md`, with source/date
      intact
- [ ] Leads with the most specific, genuinely interesting finding — not a generic
      "market is hot/cool" cliché
- [ ] Includes interpretation, not just raw data
- [ ] No manufactured urgency not supported by the actual data
- [ ] Angle matches a specific persona from `knowledge/audience.md`
