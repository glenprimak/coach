# Approved Examples (Swipe File)

This is the living library of content Glen has actually approved and published. It's the
single best training signal in this whole system — when writing something new, check
here first for the closest matching format and match its standard.

**How to use this file:** after any piece of content is approved and goes live, paste
the final version into the matching section below with the date and a one-line note on
why it worked. Over time this becomes the best possible reference for "what good looks
like" in Glen's actual voice.

The examples below are illustrative placeholders only — clearly fictional, built to show
the *shape and tone* the system is aiming for. They contain no real property facts,
prices, or claims. Replace them with real approved content as soon as it exists, and
never treat the placeholder numbers/details in them as real data.
