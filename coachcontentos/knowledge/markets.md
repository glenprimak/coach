# Markets & Neighborhoods

Neighborhood-level knowledge frameworks for Glen's core territory. These are structural
guides for what to say about each area — they are **not** pre-loaded with market
statistics, pricing, or inventory data, because those change constantly and must never
be guessed. Always pull current numbers from `inputs/market-data.md`, never from memory
or general knowledge about South Florida.

## Fort Lauderdale (Overall)

**Positioning:** The urban anchor of the territory — boating capital energy ("Venice of
America") combined with a real downtown, dining, and arts scene.

**Content angles:** downtown growth and skyline changes, beach vs. inland tradeoffs,
comparison points for relocation buyers used to a real city.

**Data to source from `inputs/market-data.md` before citing:** median sale price, active
inventory, days on market, YoY change — [INFORMATION NEEDED per current cycle].

## Las Olas

**Positioning:** Fort Lauderdale's walkable luxury corridor — restaurants, galleries,
boutiques, and residences along and just off Las Olas Boulevard.

**Content angles:** walkability as a luxury feature, "a day on Las Olas" lifestyle
content, comparison to a NYC neighborhood a relocator would already understand (frame
carefully — comparison, not equivalence).

**Property character:** mix of condos, townhomes, and single-family — confirm current
mix and price banding in `inputs/market-data.md` before stating specifics.

## Las Olas Isles

**Positioning:** The finger-island waterfront enclaves just south/east of Las Olas
Boulevard — deep-water, no-fixed-bridge ocean access, some of the most prestigious
waterfront addresses in Fort Lauderdale.

**Content angles:** dock/draft specifics tied to real listings, deed restriction
education, boating-lifestyle content, teardown-and-rebuild trend content (confirm
whether this is currently active before writing about it).

**Key facts content should never guess:** exact bridge clearances, specific island name
comparisons, price-per-square-foot — always `[INFORMATION NEEDED]` unless sourced.

## Rio Vista

**Positioning:** Historic, established, tree-lined waterfront neighborhood just south of
downtown — character homes, mature landscaping, strong sense of place.

**Content angles:** historic-home due diligence (older systems, renovation
considerations), architecture-focused content, community/family-lifestyle angle
alongside the waterfront angle, contrast with newer-construction Las Olas Isles product.

## Waterfront / Boating Lifestyle (Cross-Neighborhood Theme)

**Positioning:** Not a single neighborhood — a lifestyle thread that runs through Las
Olas Isles, Rio Vista, and other canal-front South Florida communities.

**Content angles:** no-fixed-bridge ocean access explainers, dock length/depth literacy,
marine contractor and seawall considerations, boat-to-restaurant day-in-the-life content,
insurance/flood-zone education (facts only, sourced).

## Miami

**Positioning:** The broader luxury and international-buyer market anchor — condo towers,
brand-name developments, global capital.

**Content angles:** building-specific breakdowns (only with confirmed details in
`inputs/property-details.md`), international-buyer considerations, comparison to Fort
Lauderdale/Sunny Isles for buyers deciding between the two.

## Sunny Isles Beach

**Positioning:** Branded and ultra-luxury oceanfront condo corridor between Miami and
Fort Lauderdale — strong developer/brand presence, strong rental and investment appeal.

**Content angles:** branded-residence content, rental policy and investment-yield
content (real numbers only), building amenity comparisons, oceanfront vs. intracoastal
distinctions.

## How to Use This File

1. Before writing any market-specific content, check whether the needed data point
   exists in `inputs/market-data.md`.
2. If it doesn't, either request it or insert `[INFORMATION NEEDED]` — do not estimate
   based on general South Florida market knowledge, since real numbers move quickly and
   an outdated or wrong figure damages credibility and may violate advertising
   standards.
3. Neighborhood *character* and *lifestyle* framing from this file can be used freely —
   it's the *numbers* that must always be sourced fresh.
4. As Glen provides more detail on each neighborhood (specific streets, landmark
   buildings, known comps he's comfortable referencing), add it here to make future
   content sharper.
