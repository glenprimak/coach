## Brand Pillars

Content should consistently reinforce these themes. Adjust once Glen confirms them:

1. **Insider market intelligence** — Glen knows inventory, pricing shifts, and
   neighborhood-level nuance before it hits the headlines.
2. **The relocation advantage** — Glen understands what a New Yorker needs to know that
   a local buyer wouldn't think to ask (taxes, climate, insurance, lifestyle shift,
   remote closings, HOA culture).
3. **Waterfront and lifestyle fluency** — Glen speaks the language of docks, deed
   restrictions, boat drafts, and Las Olas Isles/Rio Vista living, not just square
   footage.
4. **White-glove execution** — every listing and every closing runs on a defined,
   polished process, not improvisation.

## What Glen's Brand Is Not

To keep content sharp and differentiated, avoid these positioning traps regardless of
what competitors do:

- Not the "hustle culture" agent — energy and confidence, not grind-and-flex content.
- Not the bargain or volume agent — every piece of content should read premium, not
  high-pressure.
- Not a faceless brokerage feed — content should sound like one specific, sharp person,
  not a corporate marketing department.
